# Nexus Analytical Labs - Brand Guidelines

**Business Name:** Nexus Analytical Labs  
**Tagline:** Where Science Meets Certainty  
**Location:** San Diego, California, USA  
**Website (planned):** www.nexusanalyticallabs.com  
**Verification Portal:** verify.nexusanalyticallabs.com (core differentiator)

## Brand Positioning & Vibe
**Nexus Analytical Labs** positions itself as the modern, connective hub for trusted analytical testing in the research peptide and chemistry space.

**Core Strengths:**
- **Connection & Hub**: A central, reliable point where vendors and researchers meet verified, high-quality data.
- **Modern & Precise**: Clean contemporary design that feels forward-thinking yet deeply scientific.
- **Trust Through Transparency**: Emphasis on verifiable results via web verification codes and future portal.
- **US-Based Premium Service**: Fast, responsive, and rigorously professional — standing out from overseas or less polished competitors.

Nexus implies linkage, integration, and being the essential connection point between raw samples and confident scientific decisions.

## Color Palette

**Primary Navy** — `#0A2540` (Deep trust, authority, science)  
Used for: Headers, titles, footer bars, key text

**Accent Teal** — `#00A8A8` (Clarity, precision, modern science)  
Used for: Accents, verification banners, highlights, status indicators, icons

**Supporting Colors:**
- White `#FFFFFF`
- Light Gray `#F8FAFC` / `#F1F5F9` (backgrounds, cards)
- Dark Text `#1E293B`
- Medium Gray `#64748B` (secondary text)
- Pass Green `#059669` / `#10B981` (status pills, confirms)
- Light Green background `#ECFDF5`

## Typography (Recommended)

**Primary / Headings:** Helvetica Neue, Inter, or SF Pro Display (Bold for titles)  
**Body / Tables:** Helvetica, Inter, or Roboto (Regular 7-9pt for dense COA content)  
**Numbers / Data:** Monospace or tabular figures for alignment  
**Disclaimer / Legal:** 5.5-6.5pt Helvetica or similar

## Logo Usage

**Primary Logo:** `logos/nexus_logo_primary.jpg`  
- Features a central interconnected hub/nexus symbol integrated with analytical waveform elements — perfectly representing connection + precision.
- Use on white or light backgrounds for maximum contrast.
- Minimum size for print: ~1.3" wide.
- Clear space: At least 0.3x logo height around all sides.
- Do not stretch, recolor without approval, or add effects.

**Recommended Future Variations:**
- Horizontal lockup
- Monochrome versions
- Simplified icon-only version for favicon/app use

## COA Template Philosophy

Our COA is designed to be **visually superior**, modern, and instantly trustworthy:

- Prominent teal verification banner with unique code (immediate credibility signal).
- Clean two-column sample info cards.
- Professional results table with green "✓ CONFORMS" status pills.
- High-quality annotated HPLC chromatogram as the hero visual.
- Three clear approval signature blocks (Prepared / Reviewed / Approved).
- Strong, concise legal disclaimer protecting the lab while reassuring clients.
- One-page US Letter format — easy to review, print, and share.
- Fully editable in Adobe Acrobat Pro or any PDF editor. Designed for quick per-sample customization or LIMS integration.

This template positions Nexus Analytical Labs as the clear modern premium choice — connective, precise, and verification-first.
